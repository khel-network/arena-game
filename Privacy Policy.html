Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Data: Email, name, and profile picture when you sign in
  • Game Data: Match history, scores, and gameplay statistics
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform
  • Payment Data: Processed securely via our payment gateway (we don't store card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our games and services
  • To maintain leaderboards and match history
  • To process transactions and withdrawals
  • To detect and prevent fraud and cheating

3. Data Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers (like payment gateways) necessary to operate the platform.

5. Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing privacy@skillclash.in.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

SkillClash is strictly for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns, email privacy@skillclash.in.
