Is Skill Gaming Legal in India? Complete 2026 Guide

Published 14 September 2026 · By SkillClash Team · 6 min read

The short answer

Yes — skill gaming is legal in most of India. The Indian legal system has consistently held that games where skill predominates over chance are not "gambling" under the Public Gambling Act of 1867 and its state-level equivalents. This means games like chess, rummy, bridge, and — in the modern context — online skill games like Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect 4, and strategy duels are legal to play for real rewards in most states.

That said, the rules vary by state. Some states have specific regulations restricting or banning paid online gaming. Before playing any game with an entry fee, check your state's current regulations.

What makes a game "skill-based" under Indian law?

Indian courts use the "predominance test." A game is skill-based if it depends predominantly on the player's mental or physical ability. The Supreme Court of India has ruled on this several times, including in the landmark case of K.R. Lakshmanan v. State of Tamil Nadu (1996) which established that rummy is a game of skill.

Games fall into three categories:

  • Pure skill games: Chess, Tic-Tac-Toe, Nim, Dots and Boxes. No randomness affects the outcome. Always legal.
  • Predominantly skill games: Rummy, Bridge, Poker (depending on state), online strategy games. Legal in most states.
  • Games of chance: Lottery, dice games of pure luck, casino-style betting. Restricted or banned in most states.

The following states permit paid skill gaming with varying restrictions:

  • Maharashtra
  • Delhi NCR
  • Uttar Pradesh
  • Karnataka (with restrictions after 2021 amendments)
  • West Bengal
  • Punjab
  • Haryana
  • Rajasthan
  • Gujarat
  • Madhya Pradesh
  • Tamil Nadu (rules changed in 2022; check current status)
  • Kerala (rules changed in 2021; check current status)
  • Most other states not listed below

States with restrictions or bans on paid online gaming

As of 2026, the following states have specific restrictions on paid online gaming, including some skill games:

  • Andhra Pradesh: Online gaming for stakes restricted.
  • Telangana: Online gaming for stakes restricted since 2017.
  • Assam: Online gaming for stakes prohibited.
  • Odisha: Online gaming for stakes prohibited.
  • Nagaland: Regulated with licensing requirements.
  • Sikkim: Regulated with licensing requirements.

If you are in any of these states, do not play paid games on SkillClash or any other platform. You can still play free-to-play modes if available.

What about online skill games specifically?

Online skill games fall under the same legal framework as their offline counterparts. The Information Technology Act, 2000, regulates online platforms broadly, but does not specifically ban skill games. State-level Public Gambling Acts apply where they exist.

In 2023, the Indian government proposed the Online Gaming (Regulation) Bill to formalize the framework. As of 2026, this has not been fully enacted, but the direction is toward clearer regulation rather than blanket bans on skill gaming.

Summing up

Skill gaming is legal in most of India. The key is that the game must be predominantly skill-based, players must be 18 or older, and players must not be in a state with specific restrictions. If you're unsure about your state's rules, err on the side of caution and play free modes only.
