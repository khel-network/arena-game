How to Play Connect 4

  1. The board is a 7-column, 6-row grid standing upright.
  2. You and your opponent alternate turns. On your turn, choose a column and drop your disc — it falls to the lowest empty slot.
  3. The first player to connect four of their own discs in a straight line — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins.
  4. If the board fills without any four-in-a-row, the match is a draw and both stakes are refunded.

Connect 4 Strategy Tips

Connect 4 is a solved game — the first player has a theoretical winning strategy, but only with perfect play. In practice, most players miss it. Here's how to win more:

1. Own the center column

The middle column participates in the most potential winning lines. Dropping your first disc there is almost always correct.

2. Build two-way threats

A two-way threat is a setup where you have two winning moves at once — your opponent can only block one. This is the single most reliable path to victory.

3. Avoid playing directly under your opponent

If you stack discs under theirs, you're building their tower for them. Play to the sides instead.

4. Watch the diagonals

Most beginners lose to diagonal connections because they only scan rows and columns. Every turn, scan all four directions.

Is Connect 4 a skill game?

Yes. Connect 4 has zero randomness — every disc position is a player decision. Match outcomes depend entirely on strategy, which qualifies it as a skill-based game under Indian gaming law.
