Help Center

Welcome to SkillClash Help Center. Find answers to the most common questions below.

Getting Started

Sign up with Google in one tap — no forms, no passwords required. Just click "Sign Up" on the homepage and you're ready to play.

Games

SkillClash offers 14+ skill-based games including Tic-Tac-Toe, RPS, Memory Match, Simon Says, Dice Battle, Tap Race, Whack-a-Mole, Math Duel, Reflex Grid, and more. Entry fees range from ₹15 to ₹25 and rewards go up to ₹45.

Wallet & Payments

Add funds via UPI (minimum ₹1). Withdrawals are processed within 7-8 hours to your UPI account, with a minimum withdrawal amount of ₹150. All transactions are secure and instant.

FairPlay & Anti-Cheat

All matches are monitored by our FairPlay system. We use skill-based matchmaking, real-time move validation, and behavior monitoring to ensure every match is fair.

Tournaments

We run daily tournaments with free entry and prizes up to ₹250. Check the "Tournaments" section on the homepage for live events.

Refer & Earn

Invite friends and both of you get ₹50. Share your unique referral code from Profile → Refer & Earn. No limit on referrals.
