Is SkillClash really free to play? +

Yes. There are no entry fees anywhere on the platform — every duel and challenge is free to join. Rewards come as XP, badges, and leaderboard rank.

How fast does the leaderboard update? +

Instantly. The moment a match ends, your XP, rank, and match history sync in real time.

How does the anti-cheat system work? +

In-house anti-cheat and behaviour checks flag suspicious activity; confirmed rule-breaking results in a suspension.

Is my profile data secure? +

Yes. Accounts are protected with encrypted sign-in and your match history is only visible to you.
