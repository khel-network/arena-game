FAQ

Got Questions? We got answers.

Is SkillClash free to play? +

SkillClash is a skill-based gaming platform for users 18 and above. New users receive a ₹25 welcome bonus they can use to play their first match. Games have small entry stakes that go into the match pool, and winners receive the reward shown on each game card.

Do I play against real players? +

Yes. SkillClash only matches you with real human players. There are no bots in paid matches.

How fast does the leaderboard update? +

Instantly. The moment a match ends, your ₹, rank, and match history sync in real time.

How do I contact support? +

DM us on Instagram @skillclash.in_ for Top-Up or Withdrawal issues. Average response time is under 60 minutes.

How does the anti-cheat system work? +

In-house anti-cheat and behaviour checks flag suspicious activity; confirmed rule-breaking results in a suspension.

Is my profile data secure? +

Yes. Accounts are protected with encrypted sign-in and your match history is only visible to you.
